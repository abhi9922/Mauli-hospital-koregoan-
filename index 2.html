What the assistant can help with

Example visitor journeys for the Mauli Hospital concept.

📅

Appointment guidance

Direct patients to the existing appointment form or call the hospital. Live booking is not connected in this prototype.

₹

CMRF assistance

Explain that visitors can ask the hospital support team about eligibility, documents and the current application process. No unverified promises.

🩺

Departments & services

Help visitors find department information already published by the hospital.

🕒

OPD timing information

Show the timing information currently listed online, while advising callers to confirm doctor availability.

📍

Location & contact

Give the listed address and a phone link so patients can contact the hospital directly.

☎️

Emergency contact

Make direct hospital contact easy to find. The assistant does not diagnose symptoms or replace emergency services.

Potential production version: hospital-approved knowledge, a website widget, optional Marathi support, analytics and approved integrations. Build only after the owner confirms the need.
